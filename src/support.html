Support Isotopia

Isotopia is a pixel game for learning the periodic table. Students explore a town, meet element creatures called Elementals, and answer chemistry questions to catch them. It was built for a real high-school chemistry class, and anyone can play it at is0topia.netlify.app.

It's free for students and it's staying that way: no ads, no trackers, and student data is never sold.

What your support pays for

  • Hosting and the online database that saves students' progress
  • New Elementals — every element needs its own creature art
  • New areas, questions, and tools for teachers
